# 2.0.0.0-beta
* Ported to Minecraft 26.1.2
* EMI integration temporarily disabled (EMI is not yet available for 26.1.2)
* Copper equipment better repair now targets vanilla copper tools and armor (previously Insane's Survival Overhaul's), also in the Amethyst Repair data pack
* Added Spears better repair (0.75 materials for a full repair, as spears are crafted with 1 material)
  * Metal spears can also be repaired with the Amethyst Repair data pack
* Added Mace (0.75 Breeze Rods), Elytra (3 Phantom Membranes) and Wolf Armor (4.5 Armadillo Scutes) better repair
* Fixed Turtle Shell better repair not working (wrong Turtle Scute ID)
* Chainmail armor better repair now uses Iron Chains (renamed from Chain)
